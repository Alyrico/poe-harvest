      How good are the estimates?

      How the estimate works: lifeforce = a base amount for the plant type × an effect for each lifeforce mod × a yellow-wisp effect × the map setup (Atlas Drops %, your atlas tree's lifeforce quantity, white map or not). It's fitted on your plants with each effect pulled gently towards zero, so a mod seen a few times can't get a wild number. Effects are measured against no mod: mods that can't change lifeforce (experience, rarity, wisps released, monsters affected by wisps, fully linked, max sockets and the Tier 3 item lines) count as nothing, so a plant with only those mods drops the base amount. Stacked Decks: the Tier 3 deck line is turned into a deck count (decks per Tier 3 fauna × how many Tier 3 fauna, doubled by Duplicated) once you set the Tier 3 count in Settings, and valued at poe.ninja's price. Reroll advice: every roll you've read is a sample of what a fresh roll is worth, lifeforce and decks together; the popup says Reroll while the expected gain from rerolling is more than the chaos it costs.

      Chaos spent vs lifeforce gained, in chaos

      Every rerolled plant

      Extra = the lifeforce it dropped minus what its first roll would likely have dropped (that roll's estimate). Without luck = the final roll's estimate minus the first roll's - what the rerolling was worth on average, whatever the drop did. Lifeforce is valued at the price when the plant was finished (today's price for plants from before 22 Nov).

      For this to be right, press Numpad 1 on every roll, including the first: the first roll is the baseline, and each read after it in the same map counts 1 chaos. If you rerolled without pressing, open the plant (click its row) and correct the number of rerolls. Plants that never got a Numpad 3 result are left out.

      Mod icons in the popup

      The popup marks each mod ✔ (raises lifeforce), ✘ (lowers it) or ~ (no clear effect, or under 3%), from the data. Set a mod here to always show a particular icon, e.g. ✘ for a mod that makes the fight dangerous. Ranges include your Rich Soil bonus (×).
